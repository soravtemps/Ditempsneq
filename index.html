About Ditemps

Ditemps is a digital surprise-template platform for creating personalised birthday, anniversary, friendship, proposal and celebration experiences.

What we do

We provide ready-made interactive templates that can be personalised with names, messages, photos and other supported content, then shared through a link.

Our goal

The goal is to make digital surprises easier to create without requiring coding or design skills.
